## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an intimate, ultra-luxurious, cinematic digital experience orchestrated around deep romantic devotion and cosmic wonder. It operates at the intersection of haute couture editorial publication, fine jewelry curation, and high-end cinematic storytelling.

### Aesthetic Tone
- **Atmospheric & Immersive:** The canvas mimics an infinitely deep midnight expanse infused with subtle velvet wine shadows and shimmering golden nebulae.
- **Poetic Grandeur:** Typography commands dramatic visual balance—pairing grand, evocative high-contrast display serifs with whisper-quiet, ultra-clean grotesque sans-serifs.
- **Tactile Opulence:** Textures incorporate fine cinematic 35mm grain, dual-layered frosted crystal glass, incandescent star highlights, and razor-thin metallic gold micro-borders.

### Emotional Target
The interface must evoke an immediate sense of awe, reverence, intimacy, and exquisite exclusivity. Every interaction should feel like uncovering a bespoke, museum-grade love letter written across the cosmos.

## Colors

The color architecture is rooted in deep nocturnal darkness, lifted by rich wine-burgundy shadows and illuminated by multi-tiered metallic champagne gold and celestial starlight.

### Primary & Metallics (Champagne Gold Tier)
- **Primary Accent (`#DFBA73`):** Core polished champagne gold. Used for primary CTAs, active highlights, key iconography, and decorative monograms.
- **Gold Highlight (`#F5E1A4`):** Luminous specular gold for gradient peaks, hover states, and glowing text accents.
- **Burnished Gold (`#C99E52`):** Deeper antiqued gold for low-contrast borders, subheaders, and tactile pressed states.

### Secondary & Emotional Undercurrent (Wine & Crimson)
- **Romantic Crimson (`#8B1E3F`):** Dramatic focal points, badge fills, and intimate accent backdrops.
- **Deep Wine (`#5E1228`):** Rich supporting tone for gradient blooms and contextual state indicators.
- **Nocturne Velvet Undertones (`#1B0D14`, `#2A0E1A`):** Atmospheric ambient radial gradients, providing warmth behind content planes without brightening the deep obsidian canvas.

### Neutrals & Cosmic Radiance
- **Obsidian Black (`#050508`):** Absolute canvas base; ground-level darkness.
- **Cosmic Charcoal (`#0B0A10`):** Surface layer for elevated cards, modals, and narrative overlays.
- **Warm Ivory (`#FDFBF7`):** Editorial body typography, pure display text, and high-contrast labels.
- **Muted Bone (`#EAE5D9`):** Secondary body text and subdued captions.
- **Starlight Glow (`#FFE8D6`, `rgba(223, 186, 115, 0.25)`): Radial particle flares, ethereal outer glows, and soft lighting halos.

## Typography

The typography system relies on deliberate contrast between literary high-contrast serifs and calibrated, airy sans-serif elements.

### Editorial Display Hierarchy
- **Playfair Display** serves as the narrative voice: used for monumental introductory titles, poetic excerpts, chapter markers, and romantic quotes.
- Display styles emphasize generous line-heights and intentional letter-tightening at massive scales to retain theatrical drama.
- Italic variants of `headline-md` and `headline-lg` are reserved specifically for tender storytelling cues and highlighted personal references.

### Systematic Modern Foundation
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides functional grounding. Kept at a light weight (`300`) for body copy to prevent visual competition with serif titles.
- Labels, meta-indicators, dates, and navigation actions employ uppercase capitalization paired with extreme tracking (`0.15em` to `0.2em`) to echo couture fashion identity systems.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is architectural and theatrical, moving fluidly between panoramic storytelling spreads and disciplined high-fashion grid configurations.

### Grid Rhythm & Viewport Structure
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with 24px gutters and generous 80px side margins. Key content rarely spans more than 8 columns to protect editorial readability and negative space.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 40px margins.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 4-column layout with 16px gutters and 20px margins.

### Cinematic Letterboxing & Staging
- Full-screen chapter stages feature persistent or scroll-reactive top and bottom matte letterboxing (`letterbox-h`: 40px desktop, 20px mobile) finished in `#050508` to enforce a widescreen 2.39:1 aspect ratio sentiment.
- Generous vertical spacing (`space-3xl`, `space-4xl`) acts as an emotional pacing device, ensuring each narrative milestone breathes before the user advances.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through luminous optical layering rather than standard dropshadow elevation. The background functions as a nocturnal void, while foreground elements sit on translucent, frosted crystals illuminated by distant celestial points.

### Layer Architecture
- **Layer 0 (Canvas Void):** `#050508` overlaid with an ultra-fine 35mm film grain at 3% opacity to kill digital sterility. Subtly punctuated by ambient wine-red radial blooms (`#1B0D14` at 40% blur).
- **Layer 1 (Glass Plates):** Frosted glass surfaces with `background: rgba(11, 10, 16, 0.65)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, and a crisp 1px perimeter border tinted in gold leaf (`rgba(223, 186, 115, 0.15)`).
- **Layer 2 (Interactive Elements & Floating Modals):** `background: rgba(27, 13, 20, 0.75)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(32px)`, bordered with `rgba(245, 225, 164, 0.35)`, cast over a warm, diffused ambient shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(5, 5, 8, 0.85), 0 0 32px 0 rgba(223, 186, 115, 0.12)`.
- **Layer 3 (Celestial Flares):** Unbounded soft light pinpoints (`radial-gradient(circle, rgba(255, 232, 214, 0.25) 0%, transparent 70%)`) that float immediately behind key media artifacts to suggest backlighting.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a refined, architectural silhouette using subtle, tailored softening (`roundedness: 1`). Elements reject exaggerated bubbly roundness in favor of crisp, gallery-grade edges that honor classical picture framing.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Base Components (Buttons, Chips, Inputs):** 4px (`0.25rem`) standard corner radius, maintaining formal discipline.
- **Narrative Frames & Cards:** 8px (`0.5rem`) for large visual plates, providing just enough corner roll-off to avoid aggressive digital sharpness.
- **Full Circles:** Exclusively restricted to celestial particle motes, icon-only audio controls, and avatar medallions.
- **Decorative Gold Filigree:** 1px inset borders with micro-chamfered corners on prominent editorial pull-cards.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Celestial Action:** Built with an opulent champagne gold gradient background (`linear-gradient(135deg, #DFBA73 0%, #C99E52 100%)`), deep midnight text (`#050508`), bold uppercase typography (`label-md`), 4px border radius, and an ambient hover starlight glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(245, 225, 164, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary Ghost Action:** Transparent background framed by a hairline border (`1px solid rgba(223, 186, 115, 0.4)`), warm ivory text (`#FDFBF7`), subtle inward transition to `rgba(223, 186, 115, 0.08)` on hover.
- **Audio / Ambient Narrative Trigger:** Circular button (44px) housing a minimalist gold wireframe glyph, surrounded by a faint pulsating gold halo ring.

### Cards & Storytelling Panels
- **Editorial Memory Plate:** Combines glassmorphism (`backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`) with a high-contrast dual border: an outer 1px hairline (`rgba(223, 186, 115, 0.2)`) and an inner 1px inset margin spaced 6px apart to replicate luxury stationery embossing.
- **Cinematic Letterbox Frame:** 16:9 or 4:5 aspect-ratio containers featuring a faint inner gold vignette (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 40px rgba(5, 5, 8, 0.6)`), ensuring imagery melds effortlessly into midnight backgrounds.

### Chips & Chapter Markers
- Pill-shaped or subtle 4px tags styled in translucent wine-burgundy (`rgba(139, 30, 63, 0.25)`), bound with a `0.5px` border in `#DFBA73`. Text rendered in uppercase `label-sm` with ivory coloring.

### Inputs & Dedication Fields
- Background is set to deep obsidian slate (`#0B0A10`), bottom-bordered only (or subtly boxed) with `1px solid rgba(234, 229, 217, 0.2)`. 
- Active focus smoothly transitions the border to illuminated champagne gold (`#F5E1A4`) accompanied by an ethereal, soft-glow under-line bloom. Placeholder text appears in muted bone italics.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Ultra-refined 16px squares and circles crafted with 1px gold wireframes. 
- Selected state fills with a solid gold starburst dot or crisp champagne gold backdrop with an obsidian checkmark.

### Additional Narrative Components
- **Starlight Timeline Scrubber:** A delicate, hairline horizontal tracker representing chronological milestones, topped with glowing golden coordinate nodes.
- **Cosmic Quote Inset:** Centered, expansive Playfair italic passages flanked by whisper-thin horizontal rules tapering to a centered golden diamond glyph (`◆`).
